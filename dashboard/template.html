    Method

    • Data: Walmart M5, food category, stores CA_1–CA_4: 5,748 item × store series. Trained to 2016-05-22, tested on the next 28 days, which no model saw.
    • Models: AutoETS, AutoARIMA (statsforecast), LightGBM (one global model), Chronos-Bolt small (zero-shot), seasonal naive, and the unweighted average of AutoETS and LightGBM.
    • Claude Opus 5.5 forecast 112 series (100 random items + 12 store × dept totals) as an experiment, each run with and without names and dates.
    • Accuracy: WRMSSE, the official M5 metric, plus MAE and RMSSE per series.
    • Item and store × dept totals here are sums of item forecasts unless marked "direct".

    Limitations

    • One 28-day test window; close rankings can change in another window.
    • Claude was tested on 112 series; the blind test cannot rule out that it saw the data.
    • Sales are not demand: a stocked-out shelf records zero sales.
    • The order planner assumes perishable stock with no salvage value.
